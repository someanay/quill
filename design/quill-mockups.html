Accessibility — disclosurePlay-critical
9:41
How Quill uses Accessibility access

Quill uses Android's Accessibility service for one job: to place your dictated text into the field you're typing in, and to know when a field is focused so the bubble can appear.

Reads the focused text field only, to insert text
Skips password & banking fields entirely
Never reads your screen, logs keystrokes, or sends content anywhere

Prominent disclosure + affirmative consent, shown before the system settings deep-link (spec §8, todo M2). Decline path is real.
