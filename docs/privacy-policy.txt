THE SHORT VERSION
-----------------

Quill is a "bring your own key" (BYOK) voice-dictation app. You supply your own API key for the
speech-to-text provider of your choice. We do not operate any server that receives your audio, your
transcripts, or your API keys. Your keys are encrypted on your device; your audio goes directly from
your device to the provider you chose, authenticated with your key. Quill has no account system, no
analytics, no ads, and no telemetry.

WHAT QUILL HANDLES AND WHERE IT GOES
------------------------------------

Your provider API key(s) — one per provider you configure
  - Stored: on your device only, encrypted (Android Keystore, AES-256-GCM, non-exportable key;
    a separate encrypted slot per provider).
  - Sent to us: no.
  - Sent to third parties: each key is sent only as the authentication header to that same
    provider, never to any other provider and never to us.

Dictation audio
  - Stored: a temporary file on your device, deleted after transcription (kept briefly only if
    transcription fails, so you can retry).
  - Sent to us: no.
  - Sent to third parties: sent over HTTPS to the speech provider you selected (see "Your
    providers" below), using your key.

Transcribed text
  - Stored: inserted into the app you are typing in; optionally saved to a local, on-device
    history.
  - Sent to us: no.
  - Sent to third parties: if you enable the optional AI clean-up, the text (never the audio) is
    sent to Groq using your Groq key (see "AI clean-up" below).

Custom-word vocabulary
  - Stored: on your device only.
  - Sent to us: no.
  - Sent to third parties: if you add a word by voice, that short recording goes to your selected
    speech provider for transcription, like any dictation.

Crash reports (optional, off by default)
  - Stored: written on your device as a scrubbed text report (no audio, no transcripts, no keys).
  - Sent to us: no automatic upload of any kind.
  - Sent to third parties: only if you tap "Send" and choose an app in your own share sheet.

Analytics, advertising, identifiers
  - Not collected. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included.


KEY VALIDATION REQUESTS
-----------------------

When you save a key (or re-test it), Quill makes one small test request to that provider — a
fraction-of-a-second silent audio clip — to confirm the key works before you rely on it. Where a
provider distinguishes free and paid keys, Quill may make one additional minimal request to detect
your key's tier, solely so the model picker can hide models your key cannot use. These requests use
your key, go only to that provider, and contain no speech.


THE ONE THING QUILL FETCHES FROM US
-----------------------------------

Quill periodically fetches a small, static, cryptographically signed bundle of interface text
(wording/translations) over a read-only HTTPS request — at app start and when the app returns to
the foreground, if the "Check for updated text" setting is on (it is on by default; Settings >
Advanced). The bundle is identical for every user and the request contains no personal data; as
with any web request, the content host necessarily sees standard connection metadata such as your
IP address. Bundles are verified against a signing key built into the app, and privacy, consent,
and disclosure wording can never be changed this way — that text is locked into the app binary.


YOUR PROVIDERS ARE SEPARATE DATA CONTROLLERS
--------------------------------------------

Quill supports five provider configurations. When your audio (or, for clean-up, text) reaches a
provider, that provider's own privacy policy and terms govern what they do with it, including
retention and any model-training practices. Quill cannot control that. As of this writing:

  - Groq — https://groq.com/privacy-policy
  - OpenAI — https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  - OpenRouter — https://openrouter.ai/privacy
    (a router: your audio is forwarded to the underlying model provider you select through it)
  - Google (Gemini API) — https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms and
    https://policies.google.com/privacy
  - Custom endpoint — an OpenAI-compatible server you configure. You are responsible for that
    endpoint: Quill sends your audio and key wherever you point it.

A note on free tiers. Some providers treat free-tier API traffic differently from paid traffic.
For example, as of this writing Google's Gemini API terms state that content submitted on the
unpaid tier may be used to improve Google's products, while paid-tier content is not used that way.
Review your provider's current terms before dictating sensitive material on a free key.


AI CLEAN-UP (OPTIONAL)
----------------------

If you enable "Tidy my dictation", the transcribed text is sent to Groq (chat model) using your
Groq key and the tidied version is inserted instead. If your dictation provider is not Groq,
clean-up runs only when you have also saved a Groq key; otherwise it is skipped and the raw
transcript is used. The audio itself is never sent to the clean-up model.


HOW YOUR DATA IS PROTECTED
--------------------------

  - API keys are encrypted with a hardware-backed Android Keystore key (AES-256-GCM,
    non-exportable), one slot per provider — a key you saved for one provider is never sent to
    another.
  - Encryption in transit: all provider and content-host requests are HTTPS.
  - Audio is transient: recordings are temporary files, deleted after the transcription attempt
    (kept only on failure for retry, then removed).
  - Backups excluded: encrypted secrets, settings, and dictation history are excluded from
    Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  - Password fields are refused: Quill will not type into fields Android identifies as password
    inputs — native password fields and standard web password inputs. (A website that mislabels
    its password field may not be detectable at the accessibility layer.) Quill only ever writes
    into the text field you focused.
  - Clipboard hygiene: when a transcript must be placed on the clipboard (fallback when direct
    insertion fails), the clip is flagged sensitive and automatically cleared shortly afterwards
    (about 60 seconds).


DICTATION HISTORY (LOCAL, TIME-LIMITED, OPTIONAL)
-------------------------------------------------

If history is on (default), transcribed text is saved only on your device, with a retention window
you control (default 1 hour; options up to "forever", plus a row cap) and automatic pruning.
Private mode disables saving entirely. You can delete individual entries or clear all.


PERMISSIONS AND WHY QUILL ASKS FOR THEM
---------------------------------------

  - Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — to capture your dictation. Recording is user-initiated (bubble
    tap) and capped at 300 seconds per session.
  - Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — the floating bubble; also what lets the
    microphone foreground service start while you are in another app.
  - Accessibility service — solely to detect the focused text field and insert your dictation
    into it. It is enabled only after a separate in-app disclosure and your explicit consent,
    refuses password fields, and sends nothing to us.
  - Foreground service (microphone / special use) — to keep the bubble available and record while
    you are in another app.
  - Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — recording status, the bubble's show/stop controls, and
    "couldn't insert / re-enable" alerts.
  - Run after reboot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — to restore the bubble after a restart, if you had
    it on. No UI is auto-launched.
  - Internet (INTERNET) — to send audio/text to the provider you configured and to fetch the
    signed interface-text bundle.

Quill does not hold the restricted "ignore battery optimisations" permission; at most it opens the
system battery-settings page for you to make that choice yourself.


DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
---------------------------

  - API keys: kept until you remove them, clear all data, or uninstall.
  - Audio: deleted right after transcription (or after a failed attempt is retried/abandoned).
  - History: auto-deleted per your retention setting; deletable individually or in bulk at any
    time.
  - Everything at once: Settings > Advanced > "Clear all data & keys" wipes every stored key
    (all providers), all settings, dictation history, and your custom-word vocabulary.
  - Uninstalling the app removes all local data. Because secrets are excluded from backup,
    nothing survives in cloud backups either.

Data already sent to a provider is governed by that provider's retention policy — deletion
requests for that data go to them, under your own account.
