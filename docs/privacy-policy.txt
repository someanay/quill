Quill Privacy Policy

Updated October 14, 2026. Applies to the official Quill Android preview, package com.quill.quill.

Quill is maintained by Mr. Tikmany, an independent developer based in Mumbai, India. Privacy and support contact: quillapp@agentmail.to (mailto:quillapp@agentmail.to).

The short version

Quill uses API keys that you supply. Dictation audio is sent directly from your device to the speech provider you select. When AI cleanup is enabled, the transcript is sent to your selected cleanup provider.

We do not operate a Quill backend that receives your dictation audio, transcripts or provider keys. The app has no Quill account system, advertising or analytics SDK. Information you voluntarily send to support is handled separately, as explained below.

Information handled by the app

| Information | How Quill uses it |
| --- | --- |
| Provider keys and unfinished key-entry drafts | Stored encrypted on your device using Android Keystore and AES-256-GCM. A key is used only to authenticate with the provider or custom endpoint configured for it. |
| Dictation audio | Recorded into temporary app-cache files and sent to your selected speech provider for transcription. Successful transcription removes the recording. Failed requests may retain audio locally for retry; Android's Clear cache action or uninstalling removes cached files. |
| Transcripts | Inserted into a supported focused text field or copied to the clipboard. Saved locally if history is enabled. Sent to your chosen cleanup provider when cleanup is enabled. |
| History metadata | Local history can include the time, app label and app package associated with a dictation. It is not uploaded to a Quill server. |
| Custom words and imported contact names | Stored locally for vocabulary and word-substitution features. Importing contacts requires permission and an explicit import action. Quill does not upload your address book. A word dictated into the vocabulary feature is transcribed by your speech provider, like other dictation. |
| App settings and writing-style preferences | Stored on the device to apply your choices. The focused app's identity can select its writing style. |
| Optional crash reports | When enabled, the app stores a local report containing error/stack information and app/device metadata. Reports are not automatically uploaded. You choose whether and where to share a report. |

Your dictated text can contain personal information, including names. That text is shared with the providers involved in the dictation or cleanup operation you request.

Providers, authentication and network requests

Quill supports Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter and compatible saved OpenAI or custom configurations. Your selected speech and cleanup providers can be different.

The app can make authenticated key-validation requests and refresh provider model catalogs. These requests do not require a dictation recording. Quill does not send a recording merely to validate a newly entered key.

Provider requests use HTTPS. A provider or custom endpoint necessarily receives connection metadata, such as your IP address, along with the audio, text and authentication needed for the request. Quill cannot control a provider's retention, training practices, service availability or processing location.

Review the current terms of your chosen provider, including any differences between free and paid services:

- Groq privacy policy (https://groq.com/privacy-policy/)
- Google privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Gemini API terms (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)
- OpenRouter privacy policy (https://openrouter.ai/privacy)
- OpenAI privacy policy (https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)
- For a custom endpoint, the operator's applicable policy and terms.

There are no remote interface-text downloads in this version. Interface wording is bundled in each app release.

Accessibility, microphone and other permissions

- Microphone: records audio when you start a dictation or microphone test. The app does not start recording on boot.
- Display over other apps: displays the floating bubble and supports recording while you use another app.
- Accessibility service: identifies the focused editable field, checks its input/password characteristics and inserts text. It can read the focused field's existing text locally when needed to preserve or replace content correctly. The focused app's package and field metadata help select the destination and writing style. Quill does not provide a screen-scraping or keystroke-collection service, and does not upload other apps' screen contents to a Quill server.
- Contacts: used only for the optional, user-initiated contact-name vocabulary import.
- Notifications and foreground services: show bubble/recording controls, status and recovery messages.
- Run after reboot: can restore the bubble when enabled in your settings. It does not automatically record.
- Internet: connects to the providers you configure.

Accessibility access follows a separate in-app disclosure and your explicit consent. You can revoke permissions in Android Settings. Quill checks Android's password-field information, but incorrectly labelled or unsupported fields may not be detectable.

History, clipboard, backup and deletion

History is on by default with a one-hour retention window. Settings let you change retention, disable saving or use private mode. You can delete individual entries or clear history. Retention pruning and a row limit bound saved history; longer retention choices intentionally keep entries longer.

Clipboard fallback marks transcript clips as sensitive where Android supports it. Automatic insertion-failure clips are scheduled for a guarded clear after about 60 seconds. Explicit Copy actions or clipboard dictation may keep text until you or Android clear it. Other apps and Android may handle clipboard access according to their own rules.

An insertion-failure notification can show a short transcript preview. Android's notification and lock-screen settings control where that preview is visible. Local Android diagnostic logs can include app/field metadata and provider-operation status; these logs are not automatically uploaded to a Quill service.

The app excludes encrypted secrets, settings, history and stored crash reports from its Android backup/transfer rules. Custom vocabulary and other device-managed storage may be affected by Android backup behaviour.

Remove keys in Settings, delete history, or use Clear all data & keys to reset app stores. Android Settings offers Clear storage and Clear cache for app-private data. Uninstalling removes the app's private data. Data already processed by a provider, pasted into another app or intentionally shared is governed by that recipient's policies.

Support, GitHub and website visits

If you email us, we receive the message, sender address and any attachments you send so that we can respond. Do not email provider keys, recordings, transcripts or contact lists unless a specific private troubleshooting need has been agreed. We do not use support messages for advertising; messages may be retained while needed to handle the request and related follow-up.

Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone. Use email for privacy requests and sensitive reports. GitHub, app stores and website hosts process visits and connection metadata under their own policies; this is distinct from the Android app's lack of an analytics SDK.

Because Quill has no account or server-side transcript store, we cannot retrieve a copy of your local dictation history or delete data held by an API provider. Contact the provider for requests about its processing.

Children, changes and contact

Quill is intended for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us.

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app or support practices. The date above identifies this revision. Changes to app functionality require an app update; interface copy is bundled in releases.

For privacy questions, deletion requests concerning support messages, or concerns about this policy, email quillapp@agentmail.to. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.
