The bubble

One bubble. Every app.

Quill lives as a single floating bubble that stays legible on any screen — a white ring and soft shadow keep it visible over light or dark apps. Tap to listen, tap to send. Long-press to dictate straight to your clipboard.

It reads the focused field only to place your words, and it refuses password and banking fields entirely.

Live feedback

Your voice, drawn as ink

While you speak, Quill draws your voice as hand-inked gold strokes. The meter is real — it tracks the microphone level frame by frame, so you always know you're being heard.

Go quiet for a moment and Quill auto-stops, so a forgotten "stop" never uploads minutes of silence.

Privacy by design

Your key. Your words. Your device.

Bring your own key

Quill transcribes with your Groq, OpenAI, OpenRouter or Gemini key — encrypted on-device and sent only to that provider.

Nothing collected

No account, no backend, no analytics. Audio goes only to your provider; everything else stays on your device.

Encrypted at rest

Keys live in the device keystore, protected by AES-256. Transcripts stay local and auto-delete on your schedule.
