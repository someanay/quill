One app, every platform

The same Quill, wherever you write

Bring one key and get the same voice dictation on all your devices — a floating bubble on mobile, a global shortcut on the desktop, and the same privacy model on each.

Android iOS Windows macOS

Bring your own key

Transcribe with your own Groq, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible key — encrypted on device and sent only to that provider.

Types where your cursor is

Your words land in the focused field of any app — chat, email, docs, search. What you say is what gets typed, with a clipboard fallback when an app locks input.

Refuses secret fields

Password, PIN, and banking fields are detected and skipped — before recording and again before insertion. Quill never reads or writes a secret.

Private, no backend

No account, no analytics, no server for your data. History is local with time-based retention, and private mode keeps nothing at all.

Keys sealed by the OS

Each provider key is isolated and protected by the platform keystore — Android Keystore, Windows DPAPI, and the Apple Keychain.

Optional AI cleanup

Tidy filler and punctuation, or match a per-app writing style. Always fail-soft — a bad pass never loses your raw words.
