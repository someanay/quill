Built for Android

Tuned for how you actually use your phone

Floats over every app

A single overlay bubble follows you across chat, email, search and notes. Drag it, hide it, summon it back with a shake.

Optional AI cleanup

Tidy filler and punctuation, or match a per-app writing style. Always fail-soft — a bad pass never loses your words.

Custom words

Teach Quill the names and terms it keeps mishearing — including names imported from your contacts, kept on-device.

Private by default

Private mode keeps no transcripts. History is local and auto-deletes. Keys are sealed in the Android keystore.

Five providers, one key each

Groq, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Gemini, or any custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint. A key for one is never sent to another.

Survives aggressive phones

Built for Honor, Xiaomi, Samsung and friends that silently kill background apps. Recovery notices you on next open.

Dictate anywhere on Android

Quill for Android is free. Bring your own key — or start with a provider's free tier.
